Common Questions
As the world of Schedule I evolves, more and more questions will be asked that already have had the answers given to them in the past.

This section is to compile frequently asked questions so that you will no longer need to ask them over and over.
______________________________________________________________________


When is the next Update?
There is no confirmed ETA for future updates. Keep an eye out for posts made in the Announcements Channel in the Schedule I Official Discord Server, Tyler’s Twitter, Dev Streams, and the game’s Steam Page.


Can you transfer employees to different properties?
Yes, you can. You can transfer employees by talking to an employee and selecting the transfer to another property option.


Why is my game performance so slow?
Schedule I is not yet optimized. There are plans for it to be in the near future, but there is no current ETA for it. In the meantime, the “alternate” version of the game is there to alleviate some of those issues until a full optimization is available.


What does Quality do?
Product Quality increases the chances of successful sales and provides a monetary bonus. It is not required to give customers their preferred quality, but providing it greatly increases your chances of making a successful sale. Additionally it can provide a 15% additive bonus to sales for each level of quality above what the customers standards are. And yes, this does apply to dealers too.


What does Loyalty do?
Loyalty affects how much NPCs are willing to spend on your product; Max Loyalty causes Customers to order every day. Loyalty levels are static. If you are caught pickpocketing, lose a sale, knock customers out, or kill customers, your loyalty will decrease. 

What does Addiction do?
Addiction affects how much product they buy and how often they buy it throughout the week. The more addicted your customers are, the more willing they are to buy your product despite their product affinity. Addiction increases by a flat rate per sale pending the addiction level of your product. Addiction decays over time pending your product's addiction level.


Do product preferences matter with customers and dealers?
Product preference only affects gaining customers via a free sample, off the street deals, multi-product selling, and counter offering. Dealers do not have products they prefer selling; it comes down to which customer you are giving them.

Do effect preferences matter with customers and dealers?
Effect preferences do play a decently sized part in how much customers spend and how much product they buy. Though, it is not worthwhile to go out of your way to deal customers their preferred mix as there is not one mix in the game currently that has at least 1 preferred effect for most customers and provides enough profit to be worthwhile over other known mixes.


How do you get customers to pay more?
Each customer has their own set Client Wallet that gets gradually increased every five levels up until Kingpin, which then increases every level. As you rank up along with increasing the loyalty of your customers, they will gradually increase their budget range.


Why aren’t my Dealer’s Selling/Customer’s Buying?
There are a few factors that heavily play a role in how Customers buy Product: List Price, Product Affinity, Loyalty, and Addiction. (1) Check and make sure you aren’t pricing your product too high, refer to the Money Making Guide. (2) If you have been or are selling a different product type from what you are trying to sell, Customers will take a decent amount of time to acclimate to the new product. Stick to a Single Product Type to avoid having this issue. (3) Loyalty and Addiction have to be maintained at Blue/Green Level loyalty and 100% Addiction for the most consistent deal times. If your Customers’ Loyalty and Addiction Levels are too low, you will have to sell them products off the street for cheap in order to increase them to the point where they can do phone deals.


What is best for growing Weed and Coke?
Moisture Pots with Full Spectrum/LED Grow Lights (pending how many pots you use) is best for Weed. Air Pots with Full Spectrum Lights is best for Coke.




Are Additives worth it?
Absolutely. First off always use Fertilizer as it increases quality by 1 level and has no downsides. Additionally, Trip-Add (All three additives) is highly worthwhile for maximizing Coke and Shroom production, though it is not recommended for beginners due to the added complexity.

How does the game calculate Networth?
The game pools together the value of shelved product, vehicles, properties, businesses, on-hand cash, and online balance to calculate your total Networth. Do note that cash and product that is in your Dealers hands does not count towards Networth.


What is Seeded Mixing?
Seeded Mixing is Randomized Mixing that is determined per save. Ingredient Effects stay the same, but Effect Combinations are different (e.g., donut + donut no longer gives explosive). Any previously Discovered Mixes in your save will not change.

Employee Guides
There are currently four different types of employees that have specific uses with production as of Patch v0.4.4f10
Botanist
Botanists are employees that specialize in growing and drying plants.


Each Botanist costs $1000-$1500 upfront and $200 daily. 


Botanists can be hired from Manny in the Black Market.
______________________________________________________________________


Each Botanist requires a locker, daily pay, a supply source, and at least 1 assigned station to begin production. Botanists can have a max of 8 assigned stations that can be any combination of pots and racks.


To assign a locker to a Botanist, bring out your clipboard and click “E” on a Botanist. Then click the “None” that is next to “Locker”. This will allow you to select a locker for the Botanist.


To assign a supply source to a Botanist, bring out your clipboard and click “E” onto a Botanist. Then click the “None” that is next to “Supplies”. This will allow you to select a supply source for the Botanist.


To assign stations to a Botanist, bring out your clipboard and click “E” onto a Botanist. Then click the edit icon next to “Pots/Drying Racks”. This will allow you to select up to a mix of 8 pots/drying racks/mushroom beds/mushroom spawn station that the Botanist will handle. If you are selecting more than one station, you can hold shift and click on the pots or drying racks you want to assign.


Each Pot has their own system where you can select which seed and additive is used along with where you want the grown plant to go after it has been trimmed. To enter the selection screen, bring out your clipboard and press “E” on a pot. There you can select which seed, additive, and destination you want your pot to have. If selecting more than one pot, you can hold shift and click how many pots you want to manage.


Each Drying Rack has their own system where you can select which quality you want to have from trash -> poor -> standard -> premium -> heavenly. Each upgrade in quality takes 12 minutes (8 minutes at max temp with AC Units) to achieve. Choosing the quality and destinations for Drying Racks can be set from bringing out your clipboard and pressing “E” on a Drying Rack.

Chemist
Chemists are employees that specialize in cooking and mixing products.


Each Chemist costs $1500-$2000 upfront and $300 daily.


Chemists can be hired from Manny in the Black Market.
______________________________________________________________________


Each Chemist requires one locker, daily pay, and at least one assigned station to begin production or mixing. Chemists can have a maximum of four assigned stations that can be any combination of chemistry station, lab oven, cauldron, or mixing station.


To assign a locker to a Chemist, bring out your clipboard and press “E” onto a Chemist. Then click the “None” next to “Locker”. This will allow you to select a locker for the Chemist.


To assign a Chemistry Station, Lab Oven, Cauldron, or Mixing Station to a Chemist, bring out your clipboard and press “E” onto a Chemist. Then Click the edit icon next to “Assigned Stations”. This will allow you to select the station you want the Chemist to be assigned to.


Chemistry Stations require at least one acid, one phosphorus, and one pseudo; To assign a recipe, bring out the clipboard and press “E” on the chemistry station and select a recipe; Destination must be set to a Lab Oven.


Lab Ovens require at least one liquid meth or one cocaine base; Destination can be set to a shelf, safe, mixing station, packaging station, or brick press.


Cauldrons require at least 20 coca leaves and 1 gasoline; Destination must be set to a lab oven.


Mixing Stations require at least one mixing ingredient and one product; You can set a threshold for Mixing Stations from 1-20 that will determine how many products/mixers have to be in the mixing station at the minimum for it to start; Destination can be set to another mixing station, shelf, packaging station, or brick press.




Handler
Handlers are employees that specialize in moving items and packaging products.


Each Handler costs $1000-$1500 upfront and $200 daily. 


Handlers can be hired from Manny in the Black Market.
______________________________________________________________________


Each Handler requires one locker, daily pay, and at least 1 route OR 1 assigned station. Handlers can have at maximum 5 routes and 3 assigned stations.


To assign a station to a Handler, bring up your clipboard and press “E” onto a Handler. Then press the edit icon next to “Assigned Stations”. This will allow you to select which station you want assigned to a Handler; they can be assigned to packaging stations or brick presses.


Packaging Stations require at least one baggie and one product OR five products and one jar. Brick Presses require at least twenty products.


To assign a route to a Handler, bring up your clipboard and press “E” onto a Handler. Press the “None” in the “From” Column to select where you want the Handler to take items from. Press the “None” in the “To” Column to select where you want the Handler to take the items to.


Cleaner
Cleaners are employees that specialize in cleaning up any trash on the floor in your property.


Each Cleaner costs $500-$1000 upfront and $100 daily. 


Cleaners can be hired from Manny in the Black Market.
______________________________________________________________________


Each Cleaner requires one locker, daily pay, and at least one assigned trash can. Cleaners can have a maximum of six trash cans assigned to them.


To assign a trash can to a Cleaner, bring out the clipboard and press “E” on a Cleaner. Then press the edit icon next to “Assigned Trash Cans”. This will allow you to select which trash cans to assign to the Cleaner. Each Trash Can covers a 6x6 tile area.

Automation Guide
Throughout our own personal and extensive testing within the game as well as our own experience creating and automating various layouts, there have been numerous discoveries in regard to optimization.


Below is an in-depth info dump of all of my current knowledge regarding Layout Making and Optimized Automation.
______________________________________________________________________


Employee Mechanics Overview
_______________________________


Every Employee has virtually the exact same AI, just different applications. Botanists specifically focus on planting, watering, harvesting, and drying plants. Chemists specifically focus on cooking leaves/meth and mixing. Handlers specifically focus on moving items from point A to point B and packaging. All of these different applications function in unison to create an automated product-making powerhouse. 


Each Employee has a roughly 2.5 tile reach, meaning they can interact with employee stations (with some notable exceptions like stations that require the employee to interact with it from a specific side) and shelves through walls, other employee stations, and shelves. We can use that to our advantage by stacking those things against each other. 


Below is what you can use this mechanic with:
* Interact with shelf through shelf
* Interact with shelf through employee station
* Interact with pots and drying racks through shelf
* Interact with pots/drying racks through pots/drying racks


Each Employee has a set amount of stations/routes that can be assigned to them. The majority of the time, you will want to use every slot they have available, but there are some situations where it is better to reduce the amount of stations/routes assigned in order to increase efficiency and time saved (employees are slow af).


Below are some situations where you can use this mechanic:
* Mixing with 4 or more Mixers (use 2x more chemists and alternate between Mixers; e.g., 2 chemists for 4 mixers)
* Restocking supplies with Handlers from Loading Bays (use 4 or less routes to prevent bottlenecking and supply deprivation and save time spent walking)


Each Employee has a hitbox that interacts with placed stations and shelves around said Employee. Employees can walk through a 1 tile space (players can only walk through 2 tile spaces). You can use this mechanic to simplify your layouts to reduce time spent walking for your employees (e.g., a 1 tile space between 2 mixers facing each other, a 1 tile space between a cauldron and lab oven facing each other)
______________________________________________________________________


Botanist Routing and Setup
___________________________


When making property layouts, you want to have the vast majority of your Botanist Stations as close to the front entrance of your property as you can. This is to prevent as much time loss as possible since Botanists move around the most out of all Employees and are the most critical to map out properly.


Each Botanist Station has a hitbox that you can take advantage of when setting up a Botanist. Pots take up a 2x2 tile space and Drying Racks take up a 3x2 tile space. It is typical to create “blocks” of pots and or drying racks to simplify and move the stations as close as possible to the entrance (e.g., a “block” of 6 pots, a “block” of 4 drying racks, a “block” of 6 pots and 2 drying racks). There may be other scenarios where you want to use “lines” of stations in corners of properties to fill in any gaps close to the entrance (e.g., a “line” of 5 pots in a corner, a “line” of 3 drying racks in a corner, a “line” of pots or drying racks against a wall).
______________________________________________________________________


Botanist Stations and Supplies
______________________________


When creating layouts, there are specific ratios of stations you can use to make your Botanists as efficient as possible. Drying Racks are a MUST and SHOULD be set to Heavenly Quality always. Below are the ratios of stations and for which additive(s) WITH DRYING RACKS (WITH MAX TEMP AC UNITS) AND FULL SPECTRUM LIGHTS:

* Weed (No Additives) with Moisture Pots:
1 Pot to 2 Drying Racks
* Weed (Fertilizer) with Moisture Pots:
1 Pot to 1 Drying Rack
* Shrooms (No Additives):
2 Shroom Beds to 1 Drying Rack
* Shrooms (Fertilizer):
4 Shroom Beds to 1 Drying Rack
* Shrooms (PGR + Fertilizer + Speedgrow):
3 Shroom Bed to 5 Drying Racks
* Cocaine (No Additives) with Air Pots: 
5 Pot to 3 Drying Rack to 1.5 Cauldrons
* Cocaine (Fertilizer) with Air Pots:
3 Pots to 1 Drying Rack to 1 Cauldron
* Cocaine (PGR + Fertilizer + Speedgrow) with Air Pots:
1 Pot to 2 Drying Racks to 1 Cauldron
______________________________________________________________________


Chemist Routing and Setup
___________________________


When making property layouts, you want the vast majority of your Chemist Stations to be in the middle and far ends of your property (disregard for meth and coke layouts, have the Chemist Stations be as close as possible to the entrance). This is because Chemist Stations are very low maintenance with the exception of Mixing Stations.


Each Chemist Station has a hitbox that you can take advantage of when setting up a Chemist. Chemistry Stations take up a 4x2 tile space, Lab Ovens take up a 4x2 tile space, Cauldrons take up a 4x4 tile space, and Mixing Stations take up a 4x2 tile space. It is typical to arrange Chemistry Stations/Cauldrons and Lab Ovens one tile away from and facing each other; It is typical to do the same between Mixing Stations. Though there may be some alterations between which sides to face them pending the layout.
______________________________________________________________________


Automating Mixing
___________________


When creating layouts, properly automating your “String” of Mixing Stations is essential to churning out Mixed Product. Automated Mixing is made possible in conjunction with Handlers.


Each Mixing Station controls one singular Mixing Ingredient. When setting up Chemists for Mixing Stations, alternate Chemists between each other to increase efficiency and prevent bottlenecking (e.g., chem A to mixer A, chem B to mixer B, chem A to mixer C, chem B to mixer D). Make sure to organize your mixers in the same direction as where the next step of your mix is (e.g., mixer A with ingredient A placed beside mixer B with ingredient B) and set the destination of each Mixing Station to the subsequent one (e.g., mixer A to mixer B, mixer B to mixer C, mixer C to mixer D, etc.).
______________________________________________________________________


Handler Routing and Setup
___________________________


When making property layouts, you want your Handlers to do the least amount of walking possible to maximize efficiency and save time. Handler Routes should be set up in “Clusters”; “Clusters” are sets of Handler Routes that are centralized in a small area (e.g., handler route inputs and destinations for 1 handler set up in a singular room instead of around the entire property).


Each Handler has a set amount of routes and can be used for specific purposes. Handlers have Priority Routing, meaning that they will do the routes that are higher in the assigned list. It is typical to have handlers specialized in Resupplying Botanists/Chemists, Resupplying Mixing Stations, and Transferring Items. Any Handler can manage a packaging station or brick press.
______________________________________________________________________


Automating Resupplies
_______________________


When creating layouts, properly setting up handler routes is crucial for keeping your production fueled. Handlers are necessary for automating Botanists and Chemists.


There are two methods for resupplying Botanists/Chemists: Overflow Shelves and Loading Bays. Overflow Shelves are shelves that contain a surplus of a specific supply such as soil or cuke; you can use a Handler Route to transfer a specific supply from an Overflow Shelf to a specific Employee Station (e.g., banana to a mixing station, soil to a botanist supply source). Loading Bays are specialized marked areas that Handlers can use with their routes to unload vehicles/deliveries that are in that marked area to another shelf or Employee Station in your property; typically used to restock botanist supplies and mixing stations without taking up space inside of your property (e.g., Loading Bay 1 to Mixer A, B, C, and D for restocking mixing ingredients).

Mixing Guide
There are currently sixteen mixing ingredients that can be purchased at Gas Marts and thirty four different effects that can be made as of Patch v0.4.4f10

Money Making Guide
There are various money-making methods for early, mid, and late game in Schedule I, some more lucrative than others as of Patch v0.4.3f3

Below are the known Money-Making Methods and how to use them.
______________________________________________________________________


Smashing ATMs and Vending Machines


Utilizing the Baseball Bat that you can collect for free from the Basketball Court to take out your inner angst on the local machinery is one of the best ways you can start out your save.


Smashing an ATM or Vending Machine that you can find in various locations around the map with a fully charged swing can net you $10-$100 per Machine. Doing so to (nearly) every ATM or Vending Machine will reliably give you enough Cash to purchase the Sweat Shop from Mrs. Ming on Day 1.


Casino Gambling


The “Ride the Bus” mini-game entails you to first select the Color of the First Card (Red or Black). Then you will have to select whether or not the Second Card is of Higher or Lower placement (e.g., a Jack is Higher than an 8) than the First Card. You will then be asked to choose whether or not the Third Card is placed between the First and Second Card (e.g., a 10 is between a Jack and an 8). For the final portion, you will be asked to choose what suit (Hearts, Diamonds, Spades, Clubs) the Fourth Card will be. This Mini-Game generally rewards you well if you play properly.


The “Blackjack” mini-game entails you to choose whether or not to “Hit” or “Stand” in regard to the two Cards dealt to you and the two Cards (one faced down) dealt to the Dealer. The goal is to get to a value of 21 as close as possible without going over. Any card that is a 2 to 10 has the same value as its number. Any card that is a “Face” Card (Jack, Queen, King) is worth a value of 10. The Aces are worth either 1 or 11 (value chosen by player). “Hitting” draws you another Card from the Deck. “Standing” finalizes your hand and brings about the “Showdown” where the Dealer reveals their Face Down Card and “Hits” until they either reach 17 or higher or “Bust”. When you “Bust”, you go over the 21 value limit and forfeit your hand. This Mini-Game doesn’t reward you too well even if you play perfectly.


The “Slots” mini-game entails you spinning 3 wheels that have various symbols on them. The goal is to get lucky enough to have 3 of the same symbol lined up in a row. The payouts for getting 3 in a row depend on what symbols you hit. This Mini-Game provides nearly guaranteed profit on average.

  


Pawn Shop


Head on over to Mick’s Pawn Shop and freely sell off any *legal* items you may have for a varying amount. The payouts you can get from Mick (without haggling) vary somewhat randomly as you can see in the Mick’s Pawning Offers Spreadsheet. The general best use-case for making the most out of the Pawn Shop is by farming firearms from Police Officers by pickpocketing them while they are unconscious or dead. 


One method that has garnered an undue amount of attention is “Gold Bar Flipping”. This method entails you purchasing Gold Bars from Bleuball’s Boutique for $10,000 Credit and then “flipping” it by selling it directly to Mick when he offers an amount well over what you purchased the Gold Bar for. You would do this rinse and repeat on days that Mick is favorable to gain profit. The main and crucial downside of this method is the fact that it COMPLETELY depletes your credit which you need in order to resupply your operation or purchase placeables. You may be able to make a profit, but you will make SIGNIFICANTLY more by just selling your product.


Pick Pocketing


Pick Pocketing is an albeit menial method of accruing wealth, but it has its niche use-cases.


The Best Use-Cases for Pick Pocketing are:


Recovering Sold Product or Spent Cash
Stealing Mixing Ingredients 
Stealing Firearms from Unconscious or Dead Police Officers to then resell to Mick at the Pawn Shop


Selling Product


Selling Product SHOULD be your primary method of making money. There are numerous ways you can go about doing so, but it is generally accepted that selling Mixed Product is key. The best Early Game Mix is Green Crack mixed with Viagor. The best Mid Game Mix is Shrooms mixed with Donut + Viagor. The best Late Game Mix is Cocaine mixed with Banana + Cuke + Horse Semen + Mega Bean.


You typically want to stick with only ONE product type and ONE mix as each Customer has their own product type preference that has a noticeable sway in how they go about buying your product. Selling multiple different products does vastly more harm than good.

Cartel Guide (WIP)
With the addition of the Benzies Cartel, Hyland Point has now turned into a massive turf war. Will you join the Cartel and become their lackey? Or will you refuse to cooperate and wage war against them?


This Guide is based off of Patch v0.4.4f10
__________________________________________________________


First Encounter


Once you Unlock the Westville region and gain ALL Customers in that region OR Unlock 5 Customers and complete the “We Need To Cook” Quest, Thomas Benzies will message you, asking for a meetup. When you arrive, you will be asked to either cooperate with the Cartel or be marked for death. Whichever option you choose, you will not be able to change your decision after you follow through with it.
__________________________________________________________


Working with the Cartel


If you have chosen to work with the Cartel, you will become their personal drug mule. The Cartel will periodically message you asking for a large amount of a specific base product that you will have 3 days to complete and deposit it in the Dropbox in front of the Manor. Afterwards, you will be paid by the Cartel throwing a bundle of cash over the wall.


If you do not complete and deposit the order within the 3 days given, the Cartel will message you, warning you and giving you 1 extra day to fulfill the order with the downside of you receiving half pay.


If you fail your second chance with the Cartel, they will cut ties with you and become hostile, forcing you to fight against them for the remainder of the game.


Do note that if you work with the Cartel, you will be locked to the Northtown and Westville regions. You will no longer be able to unlock any other regions or customers during that time.
__________________________________________________________




Fighting the Cartel Intro


If you have chosen to fight against the Cartel, you will gain a hostile relationship with them. The Cartel will now occasionally ambush you around the map and during deals along with dealing to customers in your newest region. You will not be able to repair relations with them, so keep an eye out for Cartel Members dressed in Green (try not to accidentally curb stomp George Greene).


You will now have access to unlocking each region by degrading Cartel Influence. You can degrade Cartel Influence by graffitiing, cleaning Cartel Graffiti, disrupting Cartel Deals/Killing Cartel Dealers, gaining customers, and defending against ambushes. You will need to degrade Cartel Influence to at least 300/1000 in order to unlock the next region. The cartel will no longer ambush you in a region when you reach 0 Cartel Influence in said region.

Graffitiing


In order to graffiti, you will need to purchase Spray Paint from Gas Mart. You will be able to purchase 1 extra Spray Paint each level. Note that you will not degrade Cartel Influence with Graffiti if you have either not talked with Thomas Benzies or have accepted his deal.


Once you have purchased Spray Paint, hold it out and look for White Outlined Boxes on various buildings. Go up to them and press “E” to initiate the Graffiti Minigame. You can now put your artistic talents to good use and spray away! When you are finished, press “Esc” and click “Finalize”. Completing Graffiti will degrade Cartel Influence in that region by 50.


You can also find various Graffiti that were sprayed by Cartel Members that propagandize the Benzies. Using the Graffiti Cleaner from Gas Mart on them will degrade Cartel Influence in that region by 100.










Disrupting Cartel Deals and Ambushes


Throughout Hyland Point, you will be able to find various Cartel Dealer NPCs dressed in green that will roam around and complete deals with customers you have not gained yet. 


Cartel Dealers need Unclaimed Customers to make deals with, they will not deal to Customers that you have already gained. You can find Customers that are being dealt to by Cartel Dealers by going up to them; they will give a “Complete Deal” Prompt with a “This Customer is Waiting For a Rival Dealer” message. To Disrupt Cartel Deals, you just need to knock out or kill the Cartel Dealer. Doing so will degrade Cartel Influence in that region. 


You can also pickpocket drugs from knocked out/killed Cartel Dealers to gain an early/mid game advantage. You may also find Cartel Dealers occasionally going out on Smoke Breaks where you can knock out/kill them.


While you venture throughout Hyland Point, you may get ambushed by 1-4 Cartel Members that will be armed with melee weapons (and or firearms pending on your level). Ambushes are random events, players and dealers will not get ambushed every time they complete the trigger requirements. Defending yourself will degrade Cartel Influence in that region.


The Cartel will ambush you if you are alive (not in pursuit), inside the range of an ambush location, if no police are within 15 meters of a player, and not having a pending body search. Ambushes only trigger if a player or dealer completes a deal within 10 meters of an ambush location or a player enters an ambush location.
Dealers may also get ambushed by the Cartel. When one occurs, you will be messaged by your dealer stating that they got jumped and how much cash/product they lost. Your Dealers also have the chance to text you when a Cartel Deal is happening. You can decrease how much your dealer loses and increase the chances of your dealer defending themselves by arming them with melee or ranged weapons.
