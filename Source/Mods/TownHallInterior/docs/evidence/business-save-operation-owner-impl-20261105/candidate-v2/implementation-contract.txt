## Semantics to implement and test independently

1. Each explicitly synthetic process model has one shared slot. New owner facades
   cannot reset it or its early revocation. Separate model objects represent
   separate simulated processes, NOT a wired OS-process singleton or native authority.
2. Model creation captures the main managed thread; new facades inherit that
   thread identity. Except Revoke and Snapshot, off-thread methods revoke and do
   not admit intents. Snapshot and all mutation/revocation are synchronized on
   one private model lock; no external callbacks, tasks, timers or delegates.
3. Bind validates `ConstructionGate.CheckSave` for fixed TownHallTest/SaveGame_2,
   positive epoch, all seven nonempty identity labels <=160 characters without
   controls, finite nonnegative time, and bounded paths (<=32767 chars). Freeze
   canonical absolute path values without opening files. Invalid candidate before
   binding consumes no slot; an early callback/explicit revoke closes admission.
4. Bound key is `(ProcessId, 1)`; retain immutable binding forever for this
   bounded slice. Same paths/pointers/labels with different epoch or identity must
   not rebind. Every stamp needs exact key/binding, positive strictly advancing
   sequence, finite nonnegative nondecreasing time; max-value exhaustion cannot wrap.
5. Status is only `empty`, `bound-data-only`, `quarantined`; no native lifecycle
   state or proof can be established by this minimal ledger. Actual call counts
   are always zero; named effects are modeled intent/return/readback/failure bits.
6. Effects occur in enum order. A new intent requires all prior effects Returned
   and ReadbackConfirmed without Failure, no active/uncertain callbacks, and no
   revocation. Set intent at the synchronized model transition, at most once per
   effect. Duplicate/invalid ordering revokes, never clears previous bits.
7. Failed outcome or failed readback latches Failure and revokes. No outcome
   without prior intent; confirmed readback requires a prior returned outcome.
   Valid late outcomes/readbacks for already-intended effects may be recorded
   after revocation but cannot clear it or authorize another effect. Duplicate
   return/readback or undefined enum values revoke, never retry/roll back.
8. Callback enter/exit only count synthetic messages, not native dispatch or
   execution of callback bodies. Track at most64 active tickets; keep an
   uncertainty latch on invalid/unbound/off-thread/untracked entry or exit.
   Bound, same-thread valid late callbacks may be counted after revocation;
   never claim zero means quiescence. Callers can model finally with ExitCallback.
9. ObserveContext compares the candidate with frozen canonical binding; change/
   loss revokes but never replaces it. UnexpectedSave revokes without issuing a
   save or intercepting others. Telemetry (including repeated Completion/Idle/
   queue-empty hints) has no effect/proof authority. Revoke-before-intent denies
   it; intent-before-Revoke retains possible effect. Return never revives pending.
10. At bound elapsed>=60, Tick/observations revoke for diagnostic timeout only.
    Clock/sequence errors and ring overflow latch incomplete diagnostics and
    revoke. Keep <=64 latest bounded entries (Kind/Detail <=160 chars), first
    bounded revocation cause and fixed latches. Never store whole input stamps,
    payloads or SaveStrings. Snapshots must be independent read-only copies.
11. All three actual native proof strings remain unknown and permission flags
    false regardless of fake messages, even a fully successful modeled ledger.
    No IBusinessSavePort, old SaveTrial.Begin, Unity types, filesystem IO,
    registration/callback/mod wiring, extra dependencies or new production file.
